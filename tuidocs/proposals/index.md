# Proposals

Proposals are changes that people without root submitted for you to review. Nothing changes on the host until you approve one.

Each row shows the kind (stack folder, stack settings, maintenance window, notification channel or standing grant), who submitted it, when, when it expires and its state: open, approving, applied, failed, rejected, withdrawn or expired. Only open proposals can be decided. Without proposals the list says so. After the start, a notice tells you how many open proposals wait.

- {up} and {down} choose a row.
- {enter} opens the proposal to read its content and effect.
- {search} filters the rows by what you type. {back} clears the filter.
- {reload} reads the list again.
- {area} switches to another area and {quit} leaves.
