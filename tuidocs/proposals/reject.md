# Rejecting a proposal

This form asks for an optional reason, then a question confirms. Rejecting closes the proposal as rejected and nothing on the host changes. The submitter can read the reason, so keep it short and free of secrets. It may have at most 300 characters and no control characters, otherwise the form names the problem.

- {save} takes the reason and asks the final question. An empty field rejects without a reason.
- {back} cancels and keeps the proposal open.

In the final question, {yes} rejects and {no} cancels. Without root, the sudo password is asked first.
