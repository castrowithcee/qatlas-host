# Expiry of a grant

A standing grant lets a user run an action on a stack without sudo. This form sets the last day it holds, as YYYY-MM-DD, before you approve it. The date is in UTC and the grant holds until the end of that day. It may keep or shorten the expiry the proposal asked for, never extend it. An empty field keeps the expiry asked for. If the proposal asked for none, any valid date is allowed.

The date only applies to your approval and shows in the effect. The view then marks it as not the one asked for.

- {save} keeps the date.
- {next} and {prev} move between the field and the buttons.
- {back} cancels and keeps the previous expiry.
