# Choose unclear files

A folder of files gets a first commit of the files its stacks need. Unclear files, which a stack mounts writable or names as a secrets file, stay out unless you choose them. Data or a secret may be in them. The form lists each one with its reason.

Saving plans the folder again with your choice, and the questions then show the new list. A chosen file with secret content is still checked before anything is pushed.

- {toggle} ticks or unticks the file under the cursor. Ticked files go into the first commit.
- {up} and {down} move between the files.
- {save} takes the choice.
- {back} discards the form, after a question.
