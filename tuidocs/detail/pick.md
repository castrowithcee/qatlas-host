# Choosing an entry

This list holds the entries a change of the stack can apply to: secrets to delete, backups to restore, webhooks to remove, grants to withdraw or the other stacks to take versions from. The heading names the change. Choosing an entry opens the question for it, and nothing changes before its answer.

- {up} and {down} move the cursor, {pageup}, {pagedown}, {first} and {last} jump.
- {enter} chooses the entry under the cursor and opens the question.
- {back} leaves the list without a change.
- {reload} reads the stack again.
- {area} switches to another area.
