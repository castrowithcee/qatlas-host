# Run log

This screen shows the log of the run you opened, one line per entry with its time and kind. While the run is going, new lines arrive on their own and the view follows the newest one. {back} returns to the list of runs.

- {up}, {down}, {pageup}, {pagedown}, {first} and {last} scroll. {last} jumps to the newest line and resumes following.
- {log.follow} switches following the newest line on or off.
- {search} starts typing a search. {enter} keeps it and {back} clears it.
- {log.next} and {log.prev} jump to the next and the previous line with a match.
- {log.filter} opens the time period: all lines, the last count of minutes, hours or days, or a range from a start to an end. Apply shows only the lines of the period. Discard keeps the period as it was. While the form is open it takes every key.
- {log.wrap} switches the wrapping of long lines.
- {log.time} switches the time column.
- {savelog} saves the lines shown, within the period, as a new file and tells you where it went.
- {reload} reads the log again.
