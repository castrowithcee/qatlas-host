# Result of a setting

This dialog reports that a change of a setting was made, with the details of what was done, such as the branch, the head commit or the number of files. A change without details only shows its message in the message line instead.

{no} and {enter} close the dialog. The form that made the change is closed with it, and the lists behind are read again.
